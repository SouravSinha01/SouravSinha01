## About Me

I am a **PhD Scholar in Computational Biology at IIIT-Delhi**, interested in applying computational methods to understand complex biological systems.

My broader interests lie at the intersection of **bioinformatics, biological data analysis, machine learning, and scientific computing**. I use this profile to document my learning, develop reproducible workflows, and gradually contribute to open-source computational biology.

- Building stronger foundations in computational and data-driven biology
- Interested in reliable, interpretable, and reproducible research workflows
- Exploring biological networks, molecular data, and machine learning for biology
- Open to learning, collaboration, and meaningful open-source contributions
